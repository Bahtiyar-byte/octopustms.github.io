WHO BUILT IT

I'm Brandon. I built this.

Before I wrote a line of Octopus, I drove. Owner-operator, my own truck: $4,400 a month to the bank, insurance every month, diesel that could pass $15,000. I hauled under someone else's MC because brokers don't trust a new authority, and I watched a cut of every load go to people who never touched the truck.

3 AM, past Flagstaff, twenty tons behind me. A herd of deer was running alongside the truck, and I floored it. I came to at 82 mph. There were no deer.

Weeks later, on another highway, I passed a crash. The driver stood by his cab with his hands on his head.

You can be legal on paper and exhausted one hour after you wake up, with 12 hours left on your logbook. The logbook measures hours. It doesn't measure the man. And nobody in the chain — not the broker, not the carrier, not the state — could see who was behind that wheel.

We don't need more laws. We need visibility.

SECURITY

SASR (Strict Access Security Rule)

Rolling out at launch.

  • Only phones approved by the owner can enter.

  • Only the assigned driver's phone, unlocked by his face, can accept the load.

  • Add a new face to the phone and its key destroys itself.

Security and money are the same problem: whoever sees the whole load gets paid fast, and nobody can steal it.
