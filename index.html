SECURITY

SASR (Strict Access Security Rule)

Rolling out at launch.

  • Only phones approved by the owner can enter.

    Your bank doesn't let a stranger's phone into your account. Neither do we. The owner approves each phone once; every other phone is a locked door.

  • Only the assigned driver's phone, unlocked by his face, can accept the load.

    Your bank moves your money on your face. Octopus moves your loads the same way: the assigned driver's face, on the assigned driver's phone. No password can stand in for it.

  • Add a new face to the phone and its key destroys itself.

    Every approved phone carries its own key, the way a passkey does at Apple, Google and Microsoft. Add a new face to that phone and the key destroys itself. The owner approves the phone again; the thief gets nothing.

Security and money are the same problem: whoever sees the whole load gets paid fast, and nobody can steal it.
