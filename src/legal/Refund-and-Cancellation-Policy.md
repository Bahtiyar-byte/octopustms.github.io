## 1. What This Policy Covers

This Refund & Cancellation Policy ("Policy") is part of your agreement with **Digital Ronin LLC** ("Octopus", "we", "us") and covers the per-load fee charged on the Partnership plan. It is incorporated into the Terms of Service and the Carrier Agreement. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in those documents.

- **"Load Fee"** — The flat per-load fee (currently $20.00 USD) charged to a Partnership-plan carrier when a load is created on the platform.
- **"Transport"** — The point at which a load's status becomes IN_TRANSIT — a driver is moving the freight.
- **"Cancellation"** — Deleting a load from the platform before Transport begins.

## 2. The Rule

> **Cancel before the truck moves, and the $20 comes back automatically — in full, every time, no ticket, no asking.** Once the load is in transit, the fee is earned and non-refundable.

In precise terms: the Load Fee is charged when a load is created. If you cancel that load while it is still in **Created**, **Booked**, or **Assigned** status — that is, before Transport begins — the platform automatically issues a full refund of the Load Fee to the original payment method at the moment of cancellation. You do not need to contact anyone or file anything.

Once a load reaches **In Transit** status, the work you paid for has happened: the load was sourced, booked, dispatched, and a driver is on the road with it. From that point the Load Fee is earned and non-refundable, and the load can no longer be cancelled through deletion — delivered and invoiced loads carry financial records that must be voided through the proper workflow instead.

## 3. How and When the Money Arrives

- Refunds go to the **original payment method** — the card on file that was charged
- We submit the refund to our payment processor (Stripe) immediately and in full — no partial refunds, no fees deducted
- Your bank typically posts the refund within **5–10 business days**; that timing belongs to the card networks, not to us
- If refund processing fails for any reason, the cancellation does not go through — you keep the load and can retry, so you are never left having paid for a load that no longer exists

## 4. What This Policy Does Not Cover

- **Freight charges, broker rates, and settlements** — money moving between you, brokers, factors, and drivers is your business; this Policy covers only the Octopus Load Fee
- **Free-plan usage** — if you were never charged a Load Fee, there is nothing to refund
- **Charges reversed by your bank (chargebacks)** — dispute a legitimate, earned Load Fee with your bank instead of talking to us, and we may suspend the account while it is investigated; talk to us first, we are not hard to reach

## 5. Leaving Octopus

There is no subscription and therefore nothing recurring to cancel. If you close your account, loads you cancel before Transport on the way out follow the same rule — the Load Fee comes back. Fees on loads that ran are earned. There is no exit fee, and you can export your data before you go.
