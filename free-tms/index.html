Is Octopus TMS free?

The straight answer, because you searched for it: free to start, free to search, free to track. You pay $20 per booked load — and get it back if the load falls through before transport.

Free forever

  • Registration — no credit card
  • Load search across DAT and 123Loadboard, results in under a second
  • Dispatch board, driver management, truck management
  • Live tracking from the driver's phone — no hardware
  • Documents with AI reading (rate cons, BOLs, PODs)
  • Settlements and QuickBooks sync
  • English and Russian, the whole product

The only thing that costs money

$20 when you book a load. Not per month. Not per seat. Not per feature. The fee physically cannot exist without a booking behind it — and if the load is cancelled before transport, the $20 comes back automatically.

A month with 30 booked loads costs $600 — by then you've moved roughly $75,000 of freight. That's under 1% of revenue, paid only after the revenue exists. A subscription doesn't know if you earned anything.

Why not just a cheap subscription?

Because a subscription is a guaranteed charge with no guarantee of business. We built Octopus as a partner: we don't eat until you eat. Slow month — you barely pay. Empty month — you pay nothing.
