DAT loads, inside your TMS

Bring your own DAT subscription, connect it once, and stop switching windows: Octopus searches, filters and books DAT loads from the same screen you dispatch on.

  • Real broker contacts on the result, one click to call or email
  • One-click booking that creates the load in Octopus with the broker's reference carried over
  • Match alerts — new matching loads stream in live
  • "Paid at delivery" markers on factorable loads
  • Also on board: 123Loadboard search with its own connect — two boards, one window
