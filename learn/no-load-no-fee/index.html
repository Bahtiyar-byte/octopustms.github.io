How the $20 works

I paid the bank $4,400 a month for a truck whether it moved or not. That is the bill I refuse to send you. So Octopus has one price, and it fits in a sentence: $20 per load, no load no fee. Here is the long version, straight from our payment terms.

Opening an account is free

You open an account with your name and your company. No card. Nothing is charged for opening an account and nothing is charged for having one. The $20 per load is the price of the Partnership plan, and it is the only price there is.

$20 when the load is created

On the Partnership plan you save a card with Stripe, our payment processor. Your card number never touches Octopus servers; we hold a token and the last four digits. Each time a load is created on your account, that card is charged $20, once, at that moment.

Charge and load are one step. If the charge fails, no load is created and you are told immediately. Nothing accumulates, there is no invoice cycle, and there is no month-end surprise. Every charge on your statement is one load you created that day.

Cancel before the truck moves, and it comes back

Loads fall through. The broker gives it to someone else, the pickup moves, the truck breaks down. If you cancel the load while it is still Created, Booked or Assigned, before it goes In Transit, the $20 is refunded automatically to the same card, in full, the moment you cancel. No ticket, no email, no asking. Your bank usually posts it within 5 to 10 business days; that timing belongs to the card networks, not to us.

Once the load is In Transit, the fee is earned. The load was found, booked and dispatched, and a driver is on the road with it.

A dead month costs $0

There is no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel and nothing to pay in a month nobody hauls. Zero loads, zero dollars. No seats, no per-truck fee, no setup fee, no minimum, no contract. If the price ever changes, you get at least 30 days' written notice first, and nothing changes retroactively.

What the $20 replaces, and what it doesn't

Octopus replaces the cut. A dispatch service or an umbrella carrier takes a percentage of every load, whether the load paid $800 or $8,000. Octopus takes $20, and it is the same $20 on either load. The pricing calculator on the home page shows what that difference adds up to over a month and a year.

It does not replace your costs. Insurance, fuel and load-board subscriptions are yours either way. We replace the people who took a percentage for touching a screen. We don't replace the diesel.

Driving under someone else's MC?

Then your carrier pays the $20, and you see the broker's real rate confirmation on every load. Send them this page.

The paperwork

The rules above are written down in the Payment Terms and the Refund & Cancellation Policy. The refund rule is enforced by the platform itself, not by a support queue. Questions about a charge: ask@octopustms.com. A human answers.
