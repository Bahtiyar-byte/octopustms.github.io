2. What the Service Actually Is

Octopus is a transportation management platform for motor carriers. Today the Service gives you:

  • Load sourcing and booking through the integrated DAT load board
  • Dispatch, driver and truck management, including a driver mobile app
  • Document handling — rate confirmations, BOLs, PODs and the rest of the paperwork
  • Invoicing, factoring submissions, settlements and accounting exports to QuickBooks
  • Load tracking, including public tracking links you can send to a broker

What it is not, yet: Octopus is not a marketplace. Shippers and brokers do not have accounts here. Direct shipper participation is on the roadmap, and these Terms will be updated before it ships. We would rather tell you what exists than sell you a roadmap.
